Transmission is one problem wearing two costumes.

How the research connects

A central bank moves the policy rate and asks how much reaches mortgage and deposit pricing. A supervisor watches uncertainty rise and asks how much reaches loan losses and funding costs. Both questions run through the same balance sheet, and both are answered with the same machinery: identify the shock, measure what arrives, and establish whether the shortfall is a lag or a level. My work applies that machinery to Australian retail rates and to a cross-country panel of 768 to 4,847 banks across 31 to 37 countries.

02

The Pass-Through Gap

Working analysis · retail data to June 2026

Retail interest rate adjustment in Australia, 2022 to 2026. Built entirely from published RBA statistical tables. It measures what arrived; it does not model why, and it forecasts nothing.

What changed across policy cycles

Across the 2022–23 tightening, banks passed the full cash rate increase to advertised mortgage rates and 55.3 per cent of it to online savings accounts. In the tightening under way since February 2026 they have passed 106.7 per cent to online savings and 153.3 per cent to one-year term deposits. Deposit pricing has moved from lagging the cash rate to leading it.

The cleanest observation in the analysis

Two months share an identical policy rate and an identical advertised mortgage rate. Everything that differs between them is deposit pricing, so the comparison isolates it without a model.

The shortfall is a level, not a lag

Incomplete deposit pass-through is often explained as slow adjustment that eventually completes. The path through 2022–23 does not support that. The online saving rate reached 56 per cent within six months and did not improve thereafter — at twelve months it stood at 58.6 per cent, and by the end of the episode it had drifted back to 55.3. The shortfall was established early and then persisted.

Why the estimate is built this way

Retail rates move on a coarse grid

  • The standard variable rate is unchanged in 71.4% of months since January 2020; its smallest observed move is 0.10 pp.
  • Deposit series move in 5 basis point increments. A 25 bp policy step onto a 5 bp grid means a single-move ratio can only take values in multiples of 20 per cent.
  • Per-move ratios are therefore lumpy by construction, independently of bank behaviour.

So the mean of per-move ratios is not the estimate

  • One-year term deposit, 13 moves in 2022–23: mean 103.1, median 70.0, sd 148.1, range 0 to 580.
  • The per-move ratio is a ratio of small differences; its variance rises as the denominator shrinks.
  • The episode-level estimate pools the denominator and is reported throughout. For the same series it gives 101.2.

The policy-rate definition is not innocuous

  • The published record for 2022–23 is 13 increases totalling 425 bp.
  • End-of-month cash rate target reproduces it exactly. The monthly average identifies 16 moves, inventing changes in months the Board did not meet.
  • End-of-month is used throughout. Sensitivity to the alternative is reported and never exceeds 1.4 pp.

What these tables cannot answer

  • F4 and F5 report advertised rates. The discount negotiated on a housing loan is not observed, so mortgage-side conclusions are conclusions about the reference rate.
  • Deposit pass-through varies by product; three series are reported because a single headline number would mislead.
  • Retail data end June 2026. Repricing in July or early August is not captured.

Evidence base

The same transmission question, estimated at scale. Where the Australian work observes pass-through in published retail series, this establishes it econometrically across banks and countries — and identifies what attenuates it.

2026 · Published

Global economic policy uncertainty and credit risk in emerging economies: evidence from Bangladesh

Future Business Journal 12, Article 99 · Springer Nature · Open access

Estimates the response of commercial bank non-performing loan ratios to global economic policy uncertainty, 2000–2021, and identifies the conditions under which the effect is muted.

  • A one standard deviation increase in GEPU is associated with a rise of roughly 9 per cent in the average NPL ratio
  • Fiscal capacity and bank balance-sheet strength operate as attenuation mechanisms
2026 · Submitted
Under examination

Economic policy uncertainty, bank credit risk, and funding costs

MPhil thesis · Western Sydney University

Cross-country panel evidence on both sides of the balance sheet simultaneously: 31 to 37 countries, 768 to 4,847 banks, 2000–2024. Fixed effects with Driscoll–Kraay standard errors, System GMM, instrumental variable estimation using V-Dem political polarisation, and quantile regression.

  • Uncertainty raises non-performing loan ratios and reduces the cost of deposit funding
  • Consistent with safe-haven demand for bank liabilities under uncertainty — asset quality and funding move in opposite directions

04

Method & data

Estimation

  • Panel fixed and random effects with Driscoll–Kraay standard errors for cross-sectional dependence
  • Dynamic panels — System GMM (Arellano–Bond, Blundell–Bover) with instrument-count discipline
  • Instrumental variables — two-stage least squares, weak-instrument diagnostics
  • Quantile regression for effects away from the conditional mean
  • Pesaran CD testing, panel unit root testing, episode-level ratio estimation

Data

  • RBA statistical tables — F1 money market, F4 advertised deposit rates, F5 indicator lending rates
  • BankFocus (Orbis) bank-level balance sheet and income statement data
  • Refinitiv Eikon; World Bank WDI; IMF Financial Soundness Indicators
  • GEPU — Baker, Bloom and Davis policy uncertainty index; V-Dem political indicators

Tooling

  • Stata — panel estimation, GMM, IV, dataset construction, esttab output
  • Python — pandas, wbgapi, API-based assembly, chart and report pipelines
  • Excel (advanced) — financial modelling and data cleaning
  • Zotero; Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard

Standards

  • Every figure traces to a published series with a stated table and release date
  • No interpolation of missing observations — gaps are left as gaps
  • Sensitivity to definitional choices reported, not buried
  • Inputs and code hashed so results are reproducible from source
